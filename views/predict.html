ⓘFine particulate matter smaller than 2.5 microns. Most harmful pollutant — enters deep into lungs and bloodstream. Normal outdoor range: 0–60 µg/m³.
ⓘCoarse dust particles smaller than 10 microns. Causes respiratory irritation. Normal outdoor range: 0–100 µg/m³.
ⓘNitrogen Dioxide — emitted by vehicles and power plants. Irritates airways and worsens asthma. Normal urban range: 10–80 ppb.
ⓘSulphur Dioxide — from burning coal and industrial processes. Causes acid rain and lung irritation. Normal range: 0–20 ppb.
ⓘCarbon Monoxide — odourless gas from incomplete combustion. High levels reduce oxygen in blood. Normal outdoor range: 100–2000 ppb.
ⓘGround-level Ozone — formed by sunlight reacting with pollutants. Peaks in summer afternoons. Normal range: 10–60 ppb.
ⓘAmbient air temperature. High temperatures increase ozone formation and worsen pollution dispersion. Normal range: -5 to 50°C.
ⓘRelative humidity. High humidity traps pollution near the ground. Low humidity increases dust suspension. Range: 0–100%.
